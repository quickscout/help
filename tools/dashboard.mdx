The Dashboard shows drilling and leasing activity across Texas. It's free, and you don't need an account.

Open it from **Dashboard** in the header of the home page. From other pages, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Dashboard**. In Maps, click the QuickScout logo and then **Dashboard**.

## What's on it

- **Permits** and **Completions** for the latest month, with the change from the month before.
- **New leases**: how many leases started producing recently.
- **New oil**, **New gas** and **New BOE**: the first-month production of this cycle's new leases, as a daily rate.
- **Permit & completion activity** charts both by month.
- **Initial potential** shows first-month rates. Switch it between **Oil** and **Gas**.
- **New leases** lists recently started leases. Click a row to open that lease in Maps, in a new tab.
- **Top operators** and **Top counties** rank the eight most active by first-month BOE. The **Move** column shows how each one's rank changed.

## Horizontal or vertical

The **Horizontal** / **Vertical** switch at the top changes permits, completions, new leases, both charts and the new leases table.

New oil, gas and BOE, **Top operators** and **Top counties** always cover all wells.
