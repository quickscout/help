---

The Dashboard shows drilling and leasing activity across Texas. Open it from **Dashboard** in the header.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dashboard.png" alt="The statewide Dashboard" />
</Frame>

- Switch between **Horizontal** and **Vertical** wells at the top.
- Headline numbers: **Permits**, **Completions**, **New leases**, and new oil, gas and BOE.
- **Permit & completion activity** charts the trend over time.
- **Initial potential** shows first-test rates, for **Oil** or **Gas**.
- **New leases** lists recently started leases. Click a row to open it in Maps.
- **Top operators** and **Top counties** rank who is most active and where.
