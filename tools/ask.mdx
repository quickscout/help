Ask QuickScout AI answers questions in plain English. It's free with an account, doesn't use credits, and is in beta.

To open it, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or click **Search** in the header) and choose **Ask QuickScout AI**. It's also under **Products** in the page footer.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ask-ai.webp" alt="Ask QuickScout AI answering a question about transferring a lease, with the RRC and Geology modes beside the question box" />
</Frame>

Pick a mode beside the question box:

- **RRC**: forms, permits, compliance and regulations. *"Which form do I file to transfer a lease?"*
- **Geology**: formations, reservoirs and basins. *"What's the typical depth of the Wolfcamp A in Reeves County?"*

Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send, or <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line.

## Good to know

- Each question is answered on its own. It doesn't remember earlier questions, so include the full context each time.
- The chat isn't saved. The trash icon (**Clear chat**) at the top right starts over.
- You can ask up to 10 questions a minute in each mode.
- Until you ask something, the page plays a sample conversation. Your first question replaces it.
- You need to be signed in to ask.

<Note>
  To ask about what's on the map right now, such as who is drilling in view, use [Ask about the map](/maps/ask-the-map) instead.
</Note>
