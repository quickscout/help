Alerts watch an area and tell you when something new shows up there. They're included with any [paid plan](/get-started/plans).

Most people create and manage alerts in Maps. See [Alerts in Maps](/maps/alerts) for every step. This page covers the separate **Alerts page**, which lists what all your alerts have found.

## The Alerts page

The Alerts page shows everything your alerts have found. To open it, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or click **Search** in the header) and choose **Alerts**. Some pages list it as **Notifications**. It's also under **Products** in the home page footer.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/alerts-page.webp" alt="The Alerts page in Table view: every match across your alerts, newest first" />
</Frame>

Across the top you'll see how many alerts you have, and the matches, runs and failures over the last 24 hours. Then pick a view:

- **Table** lists every match from all your alerts, newest first, over the last **7d**, **30d** or **90d**. Click a row to open that run.
- **Cards** shows each alert with its **Run history**. Click a bar to see what that run matched. Each card has a switch (**Disable alert** / **Enable alert**), **Edit alert** (the pencil), and a **⋯** menu with **Duplicate** and **Delete**.

**New alert** uses the same three steps as [Maps](/maps/alerts#create-an-alert): **Area**, **Source**, **Review**. Here you can also **Add from library** to watch a county, basin or district.

Deleting an alert asks first, then gives you a few seconds to **Undo**. Its run history is kept.

You can have up to 50 active alerts.

## Access

Alerts come with any [paid plan](/get-started/plans). If the Alerts page says **Alerts isn't enabled for you yet**, click **Request access** to email us. Once it's granted, click **I've been granted access — refresh**.
