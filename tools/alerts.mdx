Alerts watch an area and tell you when something new shows up there. They're included with any [paid plan](/get-started/plans).

If you see **Alerts isn't enabled for you yet**, click **Request access**. Once it's granted, click **I've been granted access — refresh**.

## Create an alert on the map

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Alerts">
    Click **Alerts** at the bottom of the left rail, then **New**. If you have no alerts yet, the button reads **New alert**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Area: where should this alert watch?">
    Choose one:

    - **Draw on map**: click each corner, and double-click to close the shape.
    - **Use current map view**.
    - **Or whole counties**: search for counties and pick them.

    You can add several areas. They're watched together as one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Source: what should it watch for?">
    Pick what to watch for, such as new permits or completions. Add filters if you only want some filings, like one operator's. A line under the filters tells you whether they match any existing filings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review: name it and choose where it goes">
    Enter an **Alert name**. Under **Deliver to**, pick **Email**, **SMS** or both. SMS goes to the phone number on your account.

    Click **Create alert**. Alerts check for new matches every hour.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage alerts on the map

The Alerts drawer lists your alerts.

- Click an alert to expand it. The map outlines its area and zooms to it.
- An expanded alert has **Edit alert** (the pencil), **Zoom to area**, and a **⋯** menu with **Duplicate** and **Delete**.
- The switch on each alert pauses just that one.
- **Pause all** stops every alert without deleting it. **Resume all** turns them back on.

## The Alerts page

The Alerts page shows everything your alerts have found. To open it, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or click **Search** in the header) and choose **Alerts**. Some pages list it as **Notifications**. It's also under **Products** in the home page footer.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/alerts-page.webp" alt="The Alerts page in Table view: every match across your alerts, newest first" />
</Frame>

Across the top you'll see how many alerts you have, and the matches, runs and failures over the last 24 hours. Then pick a view:

- **Table** lists every match from all your alerts, newest first, over the last **7d**, **30d** or **90d**. Click a row to open that run.
- **Cards** shows each alert with its **Run history**. Click a bar to see what that run matched. Each card has a pause switch, **Edit alert** (the pencil), and a **⋯** menu with **Duplicate** and **Delete**.

**New alert** uses the same three steps as the map: **Area**, **Source**, **Review**. Here you can also **Add from library** to watch a county, basin or district.

Deleting an alert asks first, then gives you a few seconds to **Undo**. Its run history is kept.

You can have up to 50 active alerts.
