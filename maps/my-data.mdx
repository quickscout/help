Open **My Data** on the left rail. My Data is part of the [paid plans](/get-started/plans).

## My wells

Near the top of My Data, the row *My wells: correct a well's data, or add your own* holds the wells you and your team have corrected or added. Once you have some, it counts them. Click **Open** to list them, add a well, import scout tickets or a table of wells, or export them. See [My Wells](/maps/my-wells#your-wells-in-my-data).

## Formation tops

The next row, *Formation tops: your picks and your formations*, holds the tops you and your team have picked, and your list of formations: the names and colours your tops show by. Click **Open**. See [Formation tops](/maps/formation-tops#your-tops-and-formations-in-my-data).

## Upload a file

Drop files anywhere on the drawer, or click **Drop files** in the drop card to choose them. You can drop a whole folder. QuickScout reads the file in your browser.

**Accepted:** Shapefile (.zip, or .shp with its sidecar files), GeoJSON (including newline-delimited), TopoJSON, KML/KMZ, GeoPackage, FlatGeobuf, GML, GPX, MapInfo (.tab/.mif), DXF, DWG, SQLite, and spreadsheets (CSV, TSV, TXT, XLSX, ODS).

**Not yet accepted:** file geodatabases (.gdb), old Excel (.xls), Access (.mdb) and GeoParquet.

### Place it on the map

Each file gets a card. Under **PLACED FROM**, the card shows how QuickScout located the rows. A spatial file uses **Geometry in the file**. A spreadsheet can be placed several ways, so pick one:

- **Lat/long columns**. If the values look swapped, the card says so.
- A **WKT column**.
- An API number column, as **well surface holes** or as **wellbore paths**.

Choose which columns to keep under **COLUMNS KEPT**, then click **Done**.

The card then shows **ON MAP**, or **NOTHING PLACED** if no rows could be located. If only some rows couldn't be placed, the card says how many *matched no well* or *have no coordinates*, and **Review** lists them.

## Draw on the map

Click **draw on the map** in the drop card. Choose **Point**, **Line** or **Area**, draw it, then save it to **A new layer** or an existing one.

- **Point** keeps dropping points until you click **Point** again.
- **Undo last** takes off the last thing you drew, and **Clear** takes off all of it.
- Nothing is saved until you press **Save**.

## Work with a layer

Each layer in the list has a swatch for its colour, **On map** to show or hide it, and a handle to drag it up or down, which changes which layer draws on top. With the handle selected, <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> move it too.

Click a layer to open its options under it: **Solid** or **By attribute** colours, **FILL** for an area, **Zoom to**, and **Attributes**, which opens the layer in full. There, its **Attributes**, **Style** and **Source** tabs show its rows, change how it's drawn, and tell where it came from.

**Manage library →**, at the bottom of My Data, opens the full library. Each row has **View** and **Zoom to**, and its **More** has **Export GeoJSON**, **Export CSV** and **Delete**. Tick rows to give them one colour (**Set color**), export them, or delete them together.
