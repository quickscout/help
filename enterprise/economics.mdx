Open **Economics** in the rail. It values the leases in your project: **net asset value** is PV-10 less plugging liability.

## Read the page

The summary rail:

- **Net asset value** at the top.
- **Discount ladder**: the value at other discount rates. Click a rate to re-rate the page.
- **PDP reserves**, and any **Warnings** about the data.
- **Qualification**: how many leases were valued as PDP and how many *need review*, and why.
- **Provenance**: the RRC cycle, the date valued, the scope and the deck rate.

The main page:

- **Where the value sits**: the value cut **By area**, **By operator** and **By well type**.
- **What moves the number**: a tornado chart of how oil price, gas price, opex, NRI and discount rate change the value.
- **Type curves** and **Produced to date, by operator**.
- The **Leases** table. Search it, filter it to **Valued** or **Needs review**, to one area, or to horizontal or vertical wells, and choose its **Columns**. **CSV** on the table exports just the filtered rows.

## Change the assumptions

Click **Assumptions** to edit:

- **Prices & differentials**
- **Operating** costs
- **Taxes**
- **Interests** (NRI, WI)
- **Discount** rate
- **Escalation** for oil price, gas price and opex (%/yr), with a cap in years

Click **Save** and the valuation recomputes by itself. **Reset to defaults** clears your changes. **Recompute**, on the page, reruns the valuation at any time.

## Try a what-if

With two or more areas, **Narrow scope** values just some of them. Click **Run valuation**, which can take about a minute, and **Reset** to go back.

## Lease reports

Click a lease in the table to see its production and cash flow stream and its NPV profile.

- **Export** downloads a **Report (PDF)**, the **Cash flow stream (CSV)**, or the **NPV profile (CSV)**. The CSVs are greyed out when the lease has no production forecast.
- **Discuss valuation** opens a chat about the lease's numbers. It uses [credits](/enterprise/credits).
- **Add to deal** → **New deal from this…** starts a [deal](/enterprise/deals) from the lease. The rows under **Where the value sits** have it too.

**Export CSV** at the top of the page downloads the whole leases table.

<Note>
  You'll see *Nothing to value yet* when a project has no areas, or is scoped only by county, district, basin, field or abstract. Add an area of interest or an operator to value it.
</Note>
