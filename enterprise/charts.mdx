Open **Charts** in the rail. It has two parts: the **Board**, where your charts live, and **Series & decline**. Switch between them with **Board** / **Series** at the top of the page.

## Build a chart

An empty board offers starter questions, such as *How do my 2024 wells compare to 2021?*, and **Build one from scratch**. After that, click **New chart**. Either way you get the chart builder. Choose a mode:

- **Guided** asks three questions: *What are you looking at?*, *What do you want to compare?*, and *How should it be cut?* Then pick a chart type: **Auto**, **Line**, **Scatter**, **Bar**, or **P10/P50/P90**.
- **All at once** shows every option together: the dataset to start from (and **Add dataset** / **Join on** to bring in another), **Filters** on each dataset, **Group by**, **Metrics**, **Keep only** (a filter on the grouped result), **Top N series**, **Sort by** and **Row limit**.

Finish with **Add to board**.

## Work with a chart

Each chart's menu has **Edit query**, **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Colors…**, **View data**, **Export CSV** and **Remove**. Quick edits change ranges, the legend and series without reopening the builder.

**Targets**, in the bar above the board, narrows every chart to the operators or leases you pick.

## Series & decline

Plot oil and gas over time for your project. The **Water** panel is greyed out, because Texas lease production doesn't report water.

- **Lines** draws one line per **Scope rule**, **Operator**, **County** or **Lease**. **Filter targets** focuses on some of them.
- Pick a range: **All**, **15y**, **10y**, **5y** or **3y**.
- **Decline fit** overlays the fitted decline as a dashed line. **Log** switches to a log axis.
- Choose **Line**, **Bar**, **Area** or **Scatter**.
- **Totals by target**, below the chart, shows **Cumulative** or **Latest month** for each line, and exports as CSV.
