---
title: "Watchlist"
description: "Keep leases, wells, operators and fields to hand, without changing your project's scope."
---

Each project has a watchlist. It's for things you want to keep an eye on that aren't part of the project's scope. Adding something doesn't change the scope.

The watchlist is shared with everyone on the project. Watching something brings its events into [Activity](/enterprise/activity), wherever in Texas they happen.

## Add to the watchlist

- In the Maps [data tables](/maps/data-panel), tick wells or leases and choose **Add to watchlist**.
- On a [row card](/maps/row-cards), click **Watch**.
- In [Activity](/enterprise/activity), tick rows and choose **Add leases to watchlist** or **Watch operators**.
- On the workspace's **Watchlist** page, click **Add to watchlist** and search by name or ID. **Done** takes you back.

A watchlist holds up to 500 of each kind, for example 500 leases and 500 wells. If a selection from the map tables would take it past that, nothing is added. Select fewer rows.

## See your watchlist

- In the workspace, open **Watchlist** in the rail. The trash icon (**Remove from watchlist**) takes an item off. The rail shows how many items you're watching.
- In Maps, click **Watchlist** on the left rail. Filter by kind (**All**, **Leases**, **Wells**, **Operators** or **Fields**), search, sort by **Kind** or **A–Z**, and **Export CSV**. Click an item to see its figures and timeline. **Add from map selection** adds the wells you've selected on the map.

Watchlist activity appears in [Activity](/enterprise/activity) when its scope includes **my watchlist**. Watched items are also what **+ Compare** offers on [Home](/enterprise/home#production).

<Note>
  A watchlist belongs to a project. In Maps, open **Projects** on the left rail and activate a project first. People with read-only access to a project can't change its watchlist.
</Note>
