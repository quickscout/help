For the project's well inventory and the uploaded-log list, start with [Wells](/enterprise/wells).

## Upload logs

Open **Uploads** in the rail, or **+** → **Upload logs**. Drag files onto the drop zone.

- **Accepted**: LAS 2.0 and 3.0, and raster scans (TIFF, PNG, PDF), up to 200 MB each.
- Filter the list with **All files**, **Needs attention**, **Needs review**, **Processing** or **Failed**, and by kind: **LAS** or **Raster**.
- While an upload runs, **Retry failed** retries failed files, and **Clear done** clears finished ones from the progress bar.
- Each file's **⋮** menu has **Open in workspace**, **Resolve (add API)**, **Retry**, **Re-run digitization…** and **Delete**.

<Tip>
  A file marked *Needs review* usually needs an API number. Choose **Resolve (add API)** from its menu.
</Tip>

Your organization's uploads also show on the map's **Logs** layer, and open in its **Log** tab. See [Well logs on the map](/maps/well-logs).

## Analyze a log

Open **Well Logs** → **Log Analysis**. It reopens the last log you worked on. If there isn't one, **Choose a log** or **Upload a log**. **Change log**, in the log's header, switches to another.

{/* screenshot: enterprise-log — a log open in Log Analysis (needs a demo log) */}

- Open **Tool** and choose **Pick interval** (<kbd>I</kbd>), **Pick tops** (<kbd>T</kbd>), **Add top** (<kbd>A</kbd>) or **Add interval**. The Add options let you type depths instead of clicking.
- Drag on the log strip to focus a depth interval, then **Analyze**.
- **Discuss** is a chat about the log. Ask *What can I ask?* to get started. **History** keeps earlier chats, and **New chat** starts another. Chats use [credits](/enterprise/credits).
- **Report** opens **Generate report**. Set a title, who it's prepared by and for, the interval, the sections and any notes, then click **Generate PDF**.

## Cross sections

These cross sections are built from logs: their curves side by side, with the tops and intervals picked on them. Maps has a cross-section of its own, built from wells you click on the map and hung on their formation tops. See [Cross-sections](/maps/cross-section).

Open **Well Logs** → **Cross Sections** and click **Add logs**. You need two or more.

- Tools: **Cursor** (<kbd>C</kbd>), **Zoom** (<kbd>Z</kbd>), **Top pick** (<kbd>T</kbd>), **Interval** (<kbd>I</kbd>).
- **Section type**: **Structural** or **Strat**.
- Right-click a top to **Flatten on this** or **Delete top**.
- Display options: **Fill formations**, **Intervals**, **Data points** and **Lithology**.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> undoes and <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> redoes.
- **Discuss** is a chat about the section. It uses [credits](/enterprise/credits).
- **Save section** keeps it; after that the button reads **Save**. The **Saved** menu lists your sections, and has **New section** and **Delete section**.

## Formation tops

**Well Logs** → **Formation Tops** is your formation dictionary. Edits here apply to every well.

- Search the list, or filter it by basin.
- **Browse defaults** shows the standard list. **Add** copies a default into your dictionary.
- **New top** adds your own.

A top you pick on a log is a top of the well, shared with your organization: everyone in it sees the pick, and only you can change it. It shows on Maps too, on the well's card, the **Formation tops** layer and the map's cross-section, and tops picked on Maps show on the well's logs here. See [Formation tops](/maps/formation-tops).
