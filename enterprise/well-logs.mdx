## Upload logs

Open **Uploads** in the rail, or **+** → **Upload logs**. Drag files onto the drop zone.

- **Accepted**: LAS 2.0 and 3.0, and raster scans (TIFF, PNG, PDF), up to 200 MB each.
- Filter the list by **Needs attention**, **Needs review**, **Processing** or **Failed**.
- **Retry failed** tries failed files again. **Clear done** tidies the list.

## Analyze a log

Open **Well Logs** → **Log Analysis** and **Choose a log**.

- Use the depth controls to **Pick tops** or **Pick interval**, or **Add top** / **Add interval** by typing depths.
- Drag on the log strip to focus a depth interval, then **Analyze**.
- **Discuss** is a chat about the log. Ask *What can I ask?* to get started. **Past discussions** keeps earlier chats. Chats use [credits](/enterprise/credits).
- **Report** creates a printable summary of the log.

## Cross sections

Open **Well Logs** → **Cross Sections** and click **Add logs**. You need two or more.

- Tools: **Cursor** (<kbd>C</kbd>), **Zoom** (<kbd>Z</kbd>), **Top pick** (<kbd>T</kbd>), **Interval** (<kbd>I</kbd>).
- **Section type**: **Structural** or **Strat**.
- Right-click a top to **Flatten on this**.
- Display options: **Fill formations**, **Intervals**, **Data points**.
- **Save section** keeps it. **New section** starts another.

## Formation tops

**Well Logs** → **Formation Tops** is your formation dictionary. Edits here apply to every well. **Browse defaults** shows the standard list, and **New top** adds your own.
