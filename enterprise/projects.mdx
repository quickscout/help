## Create a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **New project** on the welcome screen, or **+** → **Project** in the top bar. The **+** next to **PROJECTS** in the rail opens the Projects page, which has **New project** too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Details">
    Enter a **Project name** (for example, *Midland 2026 Deal*), an optional **Description**, and an **Accent color**. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scope">
    Choose what the project covers:

    - **Areas**: draw areas on the map, or add counties, basins or districts.
    - **Operators**: the operators you're tracking.
    - **Leases**: a list of leases.

    A project covers **operators or leases, not both**. Areas can be added to either.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build my brief">
    **Build my brief** lights up once you've added at least one area, operator or lease. QuickScout then scans recent activity in your scope and writes your first [daily brief](/enterprise/home#daily-brief). When it's ready, the project opens.

    If the brief is slow, **Open the project** lets you in while it finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing is saved until you click **Build my brief**. If you close the window before then, you'll be asked whether to discard the project.

<Tip>
  You can also start a project from the map: tick leases in the [Leases table](/maps/data-panel#select-rows) and choose **New project from these**. **Add to project scope** adds them to the project you have open.
</Tip>

## Switch projects

- Use the project selector at the top left, or the **PROJECTS** list in the rail.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>0</kbd>, or **Switch project…** in the selector, goes back to the picker.
- In Maps, **Projects** on the left rail switches projects too.

To give a project a keyboard shortcut, open the project selector and choose **Manage projects** (or **All projects…** in the rail). On the project's card, click the keyboard icon (**Assign Ctrl+Shift+1**). It takes the next free number, up to nine. Click it again to remove the shortcut.

On the welcome screen, press <kbd>P</kbd> to pin a project to the top. You can pin up to four. Pins and shortcuts are remembered in your browser, so each computer has its own.

## Edit a project

Open the project selector and choose **Edit project**, or click **Manage coverage**, at the right just under the top bar. On the Projects page, clicking a project's card opens the same editor. You can:

- Change the **Name**, **Description** and **COLOUR**.
- Add or remove areas, operators and leases under **WHAT THIS PROJECT COVERS**.
- Under **ON THE MAP**, turn on **Show only this project's wells** to hide every other well on the map.

Areas save as you draw them. Everything else is saved when you click **Save**. Changing the operators or leases rebuilds the brief and the valuation.

## Share with your team

In **Edit project**, tick **Share with ‹your organization›**. Everyone in your organization can then open it. Only the project's owner and admins see this option.

People you share with may have read-only access. They'll see *You have read-only access to this project.*

On the Projects page, an **Org** badge marks a project that's shared with your organization.

## Delete a project

On the Projects page, click the trash icon (**Delete project**) on the project's card. If it's the project you have open, you'll be asked which project to switch to.
