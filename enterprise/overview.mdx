The Enterprise workspace (called **Prospect** in some places) is included with Enterprise plans. Open it from **Enterprise** in the header of the QuickScout home page.

If you see **Prospect is invite-only**, click **Request access**, or email [craig@quickscout.io](mailto:craig@quickscout.io). Once we've turned it on, click **I've been granted access — refresh**.

## Everything is scoped to a project

A project is the set of **areas**, **operators** or **leases** you're watching. Activity, wells, economics, charts and alerts all show just that project. You work in one project at a time. See [Projects](/enterprise/projects).

When no project is open, you'll see the welcome screen. Pick a recent or pinned project, or click **New project**.

On the welcome screen:

- <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd> move, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens a project, and <kbd>P</kbd> pins it to the top. You can pin up to four.
- With more than five projects, a filter box appears. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>/</kbd> jumps to it.
- **LATEST BRIEF** adds up the newest events across all your projects.

## Getting around

The rail on the left:

| Section | Pages |
|---|---|
| | **Home**: your project's [overview board](/enterprise/home) |
| **MONITOR** | [**Activity**](/enterprise/activity) and [**Alerts**](/enterprise/alerts) |
| **EXPLORE** | **Map**, **Wells**, [**Areas**](/enterprise/areas), [**Watchlist**](/enterprise/watchlist) |
| **ANALYZE** | [**Charts**](/enterprise/charts), [**Well Logs**](/enterprise/well-logs), [**Economics**](/enterprise/economics), [**Deals**](/enterprise/deals) |
| **DATA** | **Files**, **Uploads**, **Datasets** |
| **PROJECTS** | Your first eight projects, **All projects…**, and **+**, which opens the Projects page |

Alerts, Watchlist, Areas and Deals show a count beside their name. **Charts** and **Well Logs** open to show their sub-pages.

Editors can hide rail items for everyone on a project under **Workspace settings** → **Customize rail**.

## Top bar

- **Ask QuickScout** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>): ask about activity, operators and production, or jump to a well. Asking uses [credits](/enterprise/credits).
- **+ (Create)**: a new **Project**, **Alert**, **Area of interest**, or **Upload logs**.
- **Alerts** (the bell): recent alert matches across all your projects. **View all alerts →** opens the full list.
- **Help** (the question mark): **Help center** (this site), **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Contact support**, **Data disclaimer** and **Terms**.
- **Theme** (**Light**, **Dark** or **System**) and **Fullscreen**.
- The organization switcher and your user menu.
- **Back to QuickScout** (the logo) returns to the QuickScout home page.

Below the top bar is a strip showing what the project covers. Each area and operator shows its event count over the last 30 days, and the change from the 30 days before. Lease cells show —. Click a cell to see its events. **Manage coverage** adds or removes areas, operators and leases.

### Ask beside the page

The sparkle button beside the page title (**Open Ask QuickScout**) docks the same conversation at the right of the page, so you can ask while you work. The panel has **New chat**, **Open the full Ask window** and **Close Ask**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> | Ask QuickScout |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>\\</kbd> | Collapse or expand the rail |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> | Switch to a project you've [given a shortcut](/enterprise/projects#switch-projects) |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>0</kbd> | Back to the project picker |

**Help** → **Keyboard shortcuts** lists them in the app.

## Your settings

From the user menu:

- **Workspace settings**: theme, accent color, and rail customization.
- **Credits & billing**: see [Credits](/enterprise/credits).
- **Manage Team** / **Team**: your organization's members.
