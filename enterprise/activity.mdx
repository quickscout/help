## Choose what to see

- **SCOPE**, in the page bar, sets whose events you see. Click the **Watchlist** pill to include or leave out your watchlist. Open **+** to pick a source (**The project and my watchlist**, **This project only** or **My watchlist only**) or to **Narrow to an area**.
- **The window** is set in the **Summary** band: **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, **12M** or **Custom**. You can also drag across the 12-month chart. If Summary is off, click the date readout in the page bar (such as *Last 30 days*) to bring it back.
- **Search** by lease, well or API.
- **All types** and **All operators** filter by event type and operator. **Clear filters** resets them.

## Arrange the list

- Group the list with the **Day** / **Type** / **None** chips.
- **Summary** shows the window's totals above the list.
- **Rollup** opens a side panel that breaks one event type down by who is driving it.
- **Cycle times** shows how long each step takes, such as permit to spud and spud to completion. It covers the project's whole history, not just the window.
- The **View** menu has **Density**, **Columns**, **Export this list as CSV**, and **Saved views**. A saved view keeps the scope, window, filters, grouping and columns under a name. Saved views are kept in your browser.

## Act on events

Tick rows to **Add leases to watchlist**, **Watch operators**, or **Export CSV**. **Clear selection** unticks them. People with read-only access can export, but can't add to the watchlist.

Click a row to open it:

- Wells show **Wellbore**, **Tests** and **Frac**, plus **Documents** when the well has any. The Wellbore tab has a **Well report** button.
- Leases show **Production**, **Wells** and **Owners**.
- The footer has **Satellite**, **View in Maps**, and **RRC**, a link to the official record. The arrows step to the previous and next event.

## Keyboard

<kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> or the arrow keys move through the list, <kbd>x</kbd> ticks a row, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens it.
