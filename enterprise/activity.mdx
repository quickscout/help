---
title: "Activity"
description: "Every regulatory event in your project: filter it, group it, and export it."
---

Open **Activity** in the rail.

## Choose what to see

- **Scope**: **The project and my watchlist**, **This project only**, or **My watchlist only**. You can also narrow to one area.
- **Window**: **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, **12M**, or **Custom**.
- **Search** by lease, well or API.
- **All types** and **All operators** filter by event type and operator.

## Arrange the list

- **Group** by **Day**, **Type**, or **None**.
- Turn on **Summary**, **Rollup** or **Cycle times** for totals above the list.
- The view menu has **Density**, **Columns**, **Export this list as CSV**, and **Saved views**, which keep your filters under a name.

## Act on events

Tick rows to **Add leases to watchlist**, **Watch operators**, or **Export CSV**.

Click a row to open it. Wells show **Wellbore**, **Tests**, **Frac** and **Documents**. Leases show **Production**, **Wells** and **Owners**. The footer has **Satellite**, **View in Maps**, and a link to the official RRC record.
