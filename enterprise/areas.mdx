Open **Areas** in the rail. Your areas are listed on the left, each with its size, and drawn on the map on the right. Areas save automatically.

## Draw an area

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Draw an area">
    It's in the toolbar above the map.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click around your acreage">
    Click to add each corner. Double-click, or click the first point again, to finish. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    When you finish, the **Edit area** dialog opens. Give the area a **Name** and an optional **Description**. To rename it later, use **Edit name & description** on its row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Other ways to add an area:

- **Add the current map view as an area** saves exactly what's on screen.
- **Add** opens the library: pick **Counties**, **Basins** or **Districts**, or drop a file on **File**. Files can be GeoJSON, GeoPackage, KML, FlatGeobuf, or a Shapefile (.zip, or .shp with its sidecar files).

**Reference layers**, at the top right of the map, adds county boundaries and survey lines to draw against.

## Edit or delete

- Select an area, click the pencil in the toolbar (**Edit the selected area's shape**), and drag its corners.
- **Zoom to area** frames it on the map.
- **Delete area** (or **Delete the selected area** in the toolbar) removes it. The daily brief stops using it. Wells and logs aren't touched.

You can also edit areas under **Edit project** → **Areas**.

<Note>
  Only project editors can add, edit or delete areas.
</Note>

## What areas do

- The [daily brief](/enterprise/home#daily-brief) reports activity inside your areas. After you change them, the brief says *areas changed* and offers **Re-run**.
- [Alerts](/enterprise/alerts) watch your areas.
- Areas count toward the project's scope alongside its operators or leases.
