**Home** is a board of panels about your project: **OVERVIEW**, **DAILY BRIEF**, **ACTIVITY MAP**, **EVENT FEED**, the production chart, and **ACTIVITY BY TYPE**.

## Arrange the board

- Drag a panel by the grip in its header. Drag its bottom-right corner to resize it.
- **Pin panel in place** (the lock) stops it moving. Click it again to **Unpin panel**.
- **Maximize panel** enlarges it over the board. **Restore panel**, <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or a click outside puts it back.
- **Reset layout** appears once you've moved or pinned something. It puts the panels back where a new project starts, and asks first. It only affects this board.

## Overview

Totals for the project: a headline BOE per day, **WELL RECORDS**, **LEASES**, **OPERATORS**, **DAILY OIL**, **DAILY GAS** and **AREAS**. **CYCLE** is the month the rates were reported for.

## Daily brief

Each day, QuickScout writes a short brief on new activity in your project: permits, completions and other filings. The panel shows the headline, and the events it cites under **WHAT IT CITES**. Click one to select that event on the board.

Click **Read the full brief** at the bottom of the panel to open it. The full brief has counts by type, **Events in this brief**, **Recent briefs**, **Show these on the map**, and a chat about the brief. The chat uses [credits](/enterprise/credits).

Editors also see:

- **Email me** sends the brief to the project owner each day it's ready.
- **Re-run** refreshes a brief that's out of date, for example after you change the project's areas.
- **Manage brief** can regenerate the summary or re-run it from scratch.

| If you see | Do this |
|---|---|
| *…hasn't been briefed yet* and **Manage areas** | The first brief lands after tonight's run. Check the project has [areas](/enterprise/areas). |
| **Generate brief** | Events were found but not written up yet. Click it. |
| **Retry now** | The last run failed. Try again. |
| *Couldn't load the brief.* | Click **Retry**. |
| *No nearby activity in this window.* | Nothing new happened in your areas. |

## Event feed

The latest events in your project. Sort by **Newest first**, **Highest IP oil**, **Longest lateral**, **Closest to my wells** or **Operator A–Z**.

Click a row to select it. **Open well** (or **Open record**) opens it. For an event on a lease, **Add to deal** → **New deal from this…** starts a new [deal](/enterprise/deals) from that lease.

For the full list, filters and exports, open [Activity](/enterprise/activity).

## Activity map

The events on a map. After you pan or zoom, **Search this area** filters the whole board to what's in view. **Open in full Maps** opens the same place in Maps.

## Production

Your project's oil and gas over time, on a log or linear scale.

**+ Compare**, beside the page title, charts up to five [watchlist](/enterprise/watchlist) items alongside it.

## Activity by type

A count of each event type. Click a type to see its events.
