Open a project, then **Wells** in the rail. The **Wells** view lists wells in the project's scope, including wells without an uploaded log.

## Find a well

1. Search by name, API or operator.
2. Open **Filter** to narrow by well type, status or county. **Only producers** and **Only wells with logs** are quick switches.
3. Use **Columns** to choose the information shown. Click a column header to sort.
4. Click a well to open its record beside the list.

The columns can include production, depth, lateral length and log coverage. Read the basis alongside per-well cumulative production: allocated figures are estimates of a well's share of its lease.

Selections give **Ask QuickScout** context for questions about those wells. Log actions appear when the selected wells hold logs.

## Browse logs

Switch to **Logs** to browse uploaded logs, filter their status and tags, and open a log's details. This view shows **All projects**; it is not restricted to the active project's scope.

**Upload log** opens the uploader. For formats, failed uploads, picking tops and analysis, see [Well logs](/enterprise/well-logs).

<Tip>
  Want wells for a different area instead of a project's scope? Use the [Maps data tables](/maps/data-panel) and their **Scope** and **Filters** controls.
</Tip>
