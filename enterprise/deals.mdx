Deals are in the workspace rail and in the **Deals** tab of the Maps [data tables](/maps/data-panel). New deals are filed under the project you have open. Filter the list by **Project**, **Unfiled** or **All**, and by **Active** or **Archived**.

## Start a deal

Click **New deal** and answer **What are you buying?**:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/enterprise-deal-new.webp" alt="Starting a deal: What are you buying? An operator, a lease collection, or an area of interest" />
</Frame>

- **An operator**: everything an operator holds. The **Who might sell** screener ranks operators by their recent divestitures.
- **A lease collection**: a list of leases you pick.
- **An area of interest**: the leases in an area, up to 600.

### From the map

You can build a lease collection while you browse:

1. In the Leases table, tick leases and choose **Add to deal draft**. On a lease or P-4 [row card](/maps/row-cards), click **Deal draft**.
2. Open the **Deals** tab. Your leases are in the **Draft** card, which holds up to 600. Remove a lease with its **×**, or **Clear** the draft.
3. Click **Create deal…**, give it a **Name** and **Description**, and **Create deal**.

<Warning>
  Add everything you want to the draft first. Leases can't be added to a deal after it's created.
</Warning>

You can also start a deal from [Economics](/enterprise/economics#lease-reports) or the [event feed](/enterprise/home#event-feed) with **Add to deal** → **New deal from this…**. It always makes a new deal; existing deals are listed but greyed out.

## Curate the leases

{/* screenshot: enterprise-deal-curate — a deal on its Curate tab (needs a demo deal) */}

On the **Curate** tab, mark each lease:

| Button | Key | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | <kbd>A</kbd> | Accepted: in the package |
| **R** | <kbd>R</kbd> | Rejected: not buying |
| **X** | <kbd>X</kbd> | Excluded: out of scope |
| **·** | <kbd>P</kbd> | Undecided |

Each key marks the lease and moves to the next one. <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd> (or <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>K</kbd>) move, <kbd>U</kbd> undoes, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the lease.

To work in bulk:

- Filter the list with the status chips: **All**, **Undecided**, **Accepted**, **Rejected**, **Excluded**.
- Open **Criteria** to narrow by value, then **Accept all matching** or **Reject all**. Bulk actions only touch undecided leases.
- On the **Map** tab (or on the map itself, in Maps), click a lease to accept it. Click an accepted lease to put it back.

The other tabs are **Areas** (how the leases cluster), **Package** (the value of what you've accepted) and **Assumptions** (prices, costs and interests for this deal). The **NRI** chip in the deal's header sets the ownership interest.

## Reports and exports

Click **Refresh valuation** after changing leases or assumptions. In Maps it's the refresh icon.

The rest are under **Export** (the **⋮** menu in Maps):

- **Deal report & workbook** opens **Deal documents**. Set a **Project codename** and **Effective date**, pick pages, and click **Open CIM** for the Confidential Information Memorandum. On the CIM page, click **Print** and save as a PDF: it's laid out for letter paper, landscape.
- In the same dialog, **Workbook** downloads an Excel file of every lease in the package. **First 250** / **Every lease** sets how many leases the printed report lists.
- **Lease table (current filter)** downloads the leases as they're filtered.
- **Package cash flow** → **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Annual**.

## Archive or delete

**Archive this deal** moves it to **Archived**. **Restore to active** brings it back. **Delete permanently** can't be undone, and only the deal's owner sees it.
