Deals are in the workspace rail and in the **Deals** tab of the Maps [data tables](/maps/data-panel).

## Start a deal

Click **New deal** and answer **What are you buying?**:

- **An operator**: everything an operator holds. The **Who might sell** screener ranks operators by their recent divestitures.
- **A lease collection**: a list of leases you pick.
- **An area of interest**: the leases in an area.

### From the map

You can build a lease collection while you browse:

1. In the Leases table, tick leases and choose **Add to deal draft**. On a lease card, click **Deal draft**.
2. Open the **Deals** tab. Your leases are in the **Draft** card, which holds up to 600.
3. Click **Create deal…**, give it a **Name** and **Description**, and **Create deal**.

## Curate the leases

On the **Curate** tab, mark each lease:

| Button | Means |
|---|---|
| **A** | Accepted: in the package |
| **R** | Rejected: not buying |
| **X** | Excluded: out of scope |
| **·** | Undecided |

Filter the list by status, or use **Reject all**. On the map, click a lease to accept it; click an accepted lease to put it back.

The other tabs are **Areas** (how the leases cluster), **Package** (the value of what you've accepted) and **Assumptions** (prices, costs and interests for this deal).

## Reports and exports

From the deal's actions menu:

- **Refresh valuation** after changing leases or assumptions.
- **Deal report & workbook** opens **Deal documents**. Set a **Project codename** and **Effective date**, pick pages, and click **Open CIM** for a printable offering memorandum. The **Appendix workbook** is an Excel file of the first 250 leases, or every lease.
- **Lease table (current filter)** downloads the leases as they're filtered.
- **Cash flow** downloads the package cash flow, monthly, quarterly or annual.

## Archive or delete

**Archive this deal** moves it to **Archived**. You can restore it later. **Delete permanently** can't be undone.
