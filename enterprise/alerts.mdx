---
title: "Project alerts"
description: "Watch your project's areas and get an email or text when something matches."
---

Open **Alerts** in the rail, or **+** → **Alert** in the top bar. Switch between **This project** and **All projects**.

<Note>
  Alerts watch your areas, so a project needs at least one [area](/enterprise/areas) before you can create one.
</Note>

## Create an alert

Click **New alert**. One panel holds four numbered sections:

1. **Data source**: what to watch, and a **Name**.
2. **Match criteria**: which records count.
3. **Areas**: which of the project's areas to watch.
4. **Delivery**: **Email** and/or **SMS**. SMS goes to the phone on your account. You'll get at most one email a day.

Click **Create alert**. When you edit an alert later, the button reads **Save changes**.

You can have up to 50 active alerts.

## Manage alerts

The page has two tabs: **Alerts** and **Run history**.

On **Alerts**:

- Each alert has **Enable/Disable alert** and **Edit alert**. Its menu has **Duplicate** and **Delete**. A paused alert shows a **Paused** badge.
- **Pause all** switches every alert off, and then reads **Resume all**. In **All projects** it asks first, because it reaches every project.
- Deleting an alert gives you a moment to **Undo**. Its run history is kept.

On **Run history**:

- Each row is one check. **Only runs with matches** is on from the start; turn it off to see every check.
- **Replay** opens one run's matches, broken down **By operator**, **By field** or **By county**, with **Export CSV**.

The bell in the top bar shows recent matches across all your projects. The count beside **Alerts** in the rail covers just the open project.
