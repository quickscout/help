AI features use credits: Ask QuickScout, asking about the map, discussions about logs, cross sections and lease valuations, daily brief chats, and document chats.

[Ask QuickScout AI](/tools/ask), the free RRC and geology assistant, doesn't use credits.

## Check your balance

Open the user menu → **Credits & billing**. The meter shows:

- Credits left this cycle, and when they reset. Your monthly credits reset on your billing day.
- How many you've used this cycle.
- Any you've bought on top.

On a team plan the meter reads **Team credits**, shared by everyone on the team. Otherwise it reads **Your credits**.

## Top up

On **Credits & billing**, **Units** previews how many credits you'd get and what they'd cost. It starts at your seat count. Click **Continue to checkout** and confirm the quantity on the checkout page. Sales tax is added there.

- Pay by card or bank transfer (ACH). Card payments arrive at once. ACH takes a few business days to clear before the credits appear.
- Purchased credits never expire. They're only used once your monthly credits run out.

## Running out

When you're low, the meter says **Running low**. When you're out, it says **Out of credits**.

Every chat has a model picker, so you can switch to a lighter, faster model that uses fewer credits at any time. If you run out mid-chat, the chat offers those models and a **Top up credits** button.
