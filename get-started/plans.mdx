## What's free

With no account at all you can:

- Browse the map, including permits, completions and the reference layers.
- Jump to a county, abstract, district or basin, and see new leases, from the [start drawer](/maps/tour#the-start-drawer).
- Use the statewide [Dashboard](/tools/dashboard).
- Read about the Owner Portal.

With a **free account** you can also:

- Search the map by name.
- Use the [Owner Portal](/owners/overview).
- Ask [QuickScout AI](/tools/ask) questions.
- Use the signed-in [data tables](/maps/data-panel), [well reports](/tools/well-report) and [Wellbore Builder](/tools/wellbore-builder). Some data and record drawers require paid access.

## Paid plans

The **Independent** and **Company** plans unlock paid Maps features: production records, the well, lease, operator and field drawers, lookups by RRC number, [My Data](/maps/my-data) and [Alerts](/maps/alerts). The [data tables](/maps/data-panel) and [exports](/maps/exports) require sign-in; particular records and valuation data may require additional access.

| Plan | For | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent** | 1 or 2 employees | $250 / month |
| **Company** | Up to 10 employees | $500 / month |
| **Enterprise** | 10+ employees | Custom |

**Enterprise** accounts are set up with us directly. Email [craig@quickscout.io](mailto:craig@quickscout.io).

There are no contracts, and you can cancel any time.

## Subscribe

Open [Billing](https://quickscout.io/billing) and sign in to choose a plan. You can also subscribe from a Maps paywall:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in, then open something that needs a plan">
    For example, click a well on the map. You'll see **Subscription Required**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click View Plans">
    Pick **Independent** or **Company** with **Select Plan**. **Change Plan** takes you back if you picked the wrong one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay by card">
    Enter your card and billing address in the same panel. If you are eligible for a first-time trial, checkout tells you when your card will first be charged. Returning subscribers do not receive another trial.

    You'll see **Payment received**, then **Subscription active**, and you're taken back to Maps with access on. If that takes a while, click **Check again**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change or cancel

Open [Billing](https://quickscout.io/billing) with the account that holds your subscription, then **Manage subscription**. In Maps, **Profile → Manage Subscription** opens the billing portal directly.

For a failed payment, scheduled cancellation or previous plan, follow the action on Billing. See [Manage billing](/get-started/billing).

## "Isn't enabled for you yet"

- **Alerts isn't enabled for you yet** means your account has no paid plan. Subscribe to Independent or Company. If your organization should already have access, click **Request access**.
- **Prospect is invite-only** is what you see if you click **Enterprise** in the header. Enterprise is invite-only for now. **Request access** lets us know you're interested.

**Request access** opens an email to us. Once we've turned it on, click **I've been granted access — refresh**. **Back to Maps** returns you to the map.
