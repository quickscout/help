## What's free

With no account at all you can:

- Browse the map, including permits, completions and the reference layers.
- Use the statewide [Dashboard](/tools/dashboard).
- Read about the Owner Portal.

With a **free account** you can also:

- Search the map by name or number.
- Use the [Owner Portal](/owners/overview).
- Ask QuickScout AI questions.

## Paid plans

The **Independent** and **Company** plans unlock Maps in full: production data, the record drawers (wells, leases, operators, fields), the data tables, exports, and [Alerts](/tools/alerts).

| Plan | For | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent** | 1 or 2 employees | $250 / month |
| **Company** | Up to 10 employees | $500 / month |
| **Enterprise** | 10+ employees | Custom |

**Enterprise** adds the [Enterprise workspace](/enterprise/overview): projects and areas of interest, watchlists, deals, economics and daily briefs. Enterprise accounts are set up with us directly. Email [craig@quickscout.io](mailto:craig@quickscout.io).

There are no contracts, and you can cancel any time.

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open something that needs a plan">
    For example, click a well on the map. You'll see **Subscription Required**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click View Plans">
    Pick **Independent** or **Company** with **Select Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay by card">
    Enter your card in the same panel. Access turns on as soon as the payment goes through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change or cancel

In Maps, open **Profile** → **Manage Subscription**. From the billing portal you can switch plans, update your card, download invoices, or cancel.

## "Isn't enabled for you yet"

Some features are switched on per organization. If you see **Alerts isn't enabled for you yet** or **Prospect is invite-only**:

1. Click **Request access**. This opens an email to us.
2. Once we've turned it on, click **I've been granted access — refresh**.
