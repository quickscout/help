## What's free

With no account at all you can:

- Browse the map, including permits, completions and the reference layers.
- Jump to a county, abstract, district or basin, and see new leases, from the [start drawer](/maps/tour#the-start-drawer).
- Use the statewide [Dashboard](/tools/dashboard).
- Read about the Owner Portal.

With a **free account** you can also:

- Search the map by name.
- Work in the [data tables](/maps/data-panel) and [export](/maps/exports) from them. Click a row to open its [card](/maps/records).
- Use the [Owner Portal](/owners/overview).
- Ask [QuickScout AI](/tools/ask) questions.
- Open [well reports](/tools/well-report) and the [Wellbore Builder](/tools/wellbore-builder).

<Note>
  [Ask in Maps](/maps/ask-the-map) and **Write synopsis** on a card need you to be signed in, and each uses credits. [QuickScout AI](/tools/ask) doesn't use credits.
</Note>

## Paid plans

The **Independent** and **Company** plans unlock the rest of Maps:

- Records opened from the map. Click a well, lease, operator, field or county, or [select an area](/maps/selection), and its [card](/maps/records) opens; without a plan you'll see **Subscription required**.
- Lookups by RRC number in the [start drawer](/maps/tour#the-start-drawer).
- The regulatory log catalogues in the [log viewer](/maps/well-logs), and asking about a log there.
- [Cross-sections](/maps/cross-section).
- Editing [formation tops](/maps/formation-tops) and [well records](/maps/depth-records).
- [My Wells](/maps/my-wells) and [My Data](/maps/my-data).
- The [operator and field layers](/maps/operator-field-layers).
- [Alerts](/maps/alerts).
- What's behind the map's layers: injection wells, heatmap cells, frac jobs, and the [land and owners](/maps/records#land-and-owners) under any spot.
- Projects, watchlists and deals, part of the Enterprise workspace (called Prospect in some places): the project tile and **Watchlist** on the left rail, **Watch** on a card, **Add to watchlist**, **Add to project scope**, **New project from these** and **Add to deal draft** on ticked table rows, and the **Deals** tab.

Particular records and valuation data may require additional access.

| Plan | For | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent** | 1 or 2 employees | $250 / month |
| **Company** | Up to 10 employees | $500 / month |
| **Enterprise** | 10+ employees | Custom |

**Enterprise** accounts are set up with us directly. Email [craig@quickscout.io](mailto:craig@quickscout.io).

There are no contracts, and you can cancel any time.

## Subscribe

Open [Billing](https://quickscout.io/billing) and sign in to choose a plan, or subscribe without leaving Maps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plans">
    Sign in, then open **Profile** → **See plans**.

    Or open something that needs a plan, such as **Alerts** on the left rail. Under **Subscription required**, click **View plans**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Pick **Independent** or **Company** with **Select plan**. **Change plan** takes you back if you picked the wrong one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay by card">
    Enter your card and billing address in the same panel. If you are eligible for a first-time trial, checkout tells you when your card will first be charged. Returning subscribers do not receive another trial.

    You'll see **Payment received**, then **Subscription active**. Maps opens again where you were: the same view, with the record or drawer you had open, now unlocked. If that takes a while, click **Check again**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change or cancel

Open [your account](https://quickscout.io/billing) and use **Change plan**, **Update card** or **Cancel plan**. In Maps, open your account menu and choose **Billing**.

For a failed payment, scheduled cancellation or previous plan, follow the action on your account page. See [Your account](/get-started/billing).

## "Isn't enabled for you yet"

- **Alerts isn't enabled for you yet** means your account has no paid plan. Subscribe to Independent or Company. If your organization should already have access, click **Request access**.
- **Prospect comes with a plan** is what you see if you open the Enterprise workspace without a paid plan. **See plans** takes you to [Billing](/get-started/billing) to subscribe; **Email us** asks us to set up your team. If your organization's trial has ended, the page says so instead.

**Request access** and **Email us** open an email to us. Once we've turned it on, click **I've been granted access — refresh**. **Back to Maps** returns you to the map.
