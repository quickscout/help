Open [quickscout.io/billing](https://quickscout.io/billing) and sign in. In Maps, open your account menu and choose **Manage account** or **Billing**. The page shows what you have, what it costs and who pays for it, and everything you can do about it is on the same page.

Nothing changes on one press. Each button first tells you what will happen, who is told and whether it can be undone, and only then asks you to confirm.

## Your plan

| What you see | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Free account** | Choose **Independent** or **Company**. See [Plans & access](/get-started/plans). |
| Your plan, with its next charge | **Change plan**, **Update card**, **Cancel plan**. |
| **Ends ‹date›** | **Keep my plan** undoes the cancellation, with nothing to re-enter. |
| **Card declined** | **Update card** puts a new card on your plan and pays what's owed. You keep everything while we try again. |
| **Paused** | Your plan is paused until it's paid. **Update card** or **Pay the open invoice** brings it all back. |
| **Ended** | Your work is still here. **Come back** on the plan you had, or choose another. |
| A plan that comes with your team | There's nothing for you to pay. Your team's admin manages it. |
| Free access from QuickScout | There's nothing to pay. If it has an end date and you sign up early, your first charge is the day it would have ended. |

## Move from Alerts to Full

Under **Upgrade to Full**, choose **Independent** for yourself or **Company** for your team. Full opens right away, your alerts carry on, and billing starts that day.

## Cancel

**Cancel plan** keeps everything until the end of what you've paid for, and your card isn't charged again. Your work is kept. If you change your mind before then, press **Keep my plan**.

If your card is already failing, your paid time is over, so cancelling ends your plan that day.

## Your card, address and invoices

**Update card** opens Stripe's secure page, and your next charge goes to the new card. Your invoices are listed under **Payment**, each with a PDF. To change your billing address or tax details, use the link above them.

## Your team

Everyone on a team shares its plan and its Ask credits. The page lists every team you're on.

- **Switch to this team** works in that team: its projects and credits take the place of the ones you were using. Your own projects stay with you.
- **Leave team** takes you off it. A team's only admin has to make someone else an admin first.
- An admin can invite people, remove them, and press **Make admin** beside someone. Each invitation holds a seat until it's accepted or cancelled.
- A team's admin signs the team up, changes its card and sees its invoices. If your card pays for a team and you leave, it keeps paying until another admin replaces it.

## Ask credits

With Full, **Ask credits** shows what's left this month, whether they're yours or shared with your team, and when they reset. **Buy more credits** goes to Stripe's secure page, where you choose how many. Credits you buy never run out, and they're used after the month's.

## Your details and alerts

**Edit my details** changes your name, email, mobile and password. Under **Alerts**, **Change text number** sets the number alert texts go to, and **Manage alerts** opens your alerts.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** ends any plan of your own that day, with no refund, and you're signed out at once. Your saved work stays on our servers, out of reach, until you ask us to erase it by emailing [craig@quickscout.io](mailto:craig@quickscout.io). To stop paying and keep everything until your paid time ends, use **Cancel plan** instead.

## Paid but still locked out?

Reload the page with the same signed-in account and let it load. If it shows your plan but Maps still asks you to subscribe, refresh Maps. If that doesn't fix it, email [craig@quickscout.io](mailto:craig@quickscout.io) with the address you signed up with.

If your billing can't be read, press **Try again**. An error here doesn't change your plan.
