title: "Owner Portal"
description: "Free for mineral and royalty owners: production, valuation and offers for the interests you own."
---

The Owner Portal is free. Sign in and open **Owner Portal** from the header.

## Find your properties

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Search">
    Go to the **Search** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by owner number">
    Under **Search for Your Producing Mineral Properties**, choose your **County** and enter your **Owner Number**, then click **Search Properties**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them">
    Add the ones that are yours. They appear under **Properties**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Your owner number is on your county property tax statement.
</Tip>

Own a working interest instead? Use **Search For Your Working Interest Properties** on the same tab.

You can also add a property from the map: open its lease and click **Claim Your Interest**.

## What you'll see

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Properties** | Your properties and their production. **Download Report** or **Download CSV** to keep a copy. |
| **Valuation** | An estimate of what your interests are worth. |
| **Events** | Activity on your properties. |
| **My Offers** | Offers you've received. |
