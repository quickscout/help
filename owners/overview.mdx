The Owner Portal is free. [Sign in](/get-started/sign-in), then click **Owner Portal** in the header of the QuickScout home page.

<Note>
  Use the **Owner Portal** link in the site header. The **Owner Portal** button inside Maps is part of the paid Maps plans.
</Note>

## Find your properties

Properties are added from the **Search** tab, at the right of the tab row. **Add a property** and **Add Properties** take you there too.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/owners-search.webp" alt="The Search tab of the Owner Portal, with the county and owner number search" />
</Frame>

### Mineral and royalty interests

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search by owner number">
    Under **Search for Your Producing Mineral Properties**, choose your **County** and enter your **Owner Number**, then click **Search Properties**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the ones that are yours">
    Tick your properties and click **Add to My Properties**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save them">
    On the **Properties** tab they're listed under **Pending Properties**. Click **Save All to My Account** to keep them. Until you do, they aren't saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Your owner number is on your county property tax statement. It's usually different in each county.
</Tip>

### Working interests

Under **Search For Your Working Interest Properties**:

1. Search by **Operator Name**, **Lease Name** or **Counties**, and click **Search**.
2. Click **Add Claim** on your lease.
3. Enter your **Gross Decimal Interest**. Leave **Offer for Sale** on if you'd like to receive offers. There's no obligation to sell.
4. Click **Submit Claim**.

### From the map

If you have a Maps plan, open a lease on the map and click **Claim Your Interest**. Choose **Royalty Interest** or **Working Interest**.

## What you'll see

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/owners-portal.webp" alt="The Properties tab of the Owner Portal: the tabs, your totals, and the downloads" />
</Frame>

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Properties** | Your properties with their latest month of production, and your share of it. Lease names open the lease in Maps. |
| **Financials** | Not available yet. The tab is greyed out. |
| **Valuation** | An estimate of what your interests are worth. Change the prices, tax rates, months and discount rate to see how the estimate moves. |
| **Events** | Completions filed on your leases. |
| **My Offers** | Offers buyers have sent you, with each buyer's email and phone number so you can reply. |

The county menu at the top starts on **All Counties**. Pick a county to narrow Properties, Valuation and Events, and your downloads, to just that county.

<Note>
  Public production data runs up to three months behind.
</Note>

## Download a copy

On **Properties**:

- **Download Report** opens a preview. Click **Download PDF** to save it.
- **Download CSV** saves the table.

## Offers

**Accepting** in the **Offers** column means buyers can send you offers on that property. There's no obligation to sell.

To change it, open the **⋮** menu on the property's row and choose **Stop Accepting Offers** or **Start Accepting Offers**. You can switch it back at any time.

The same menu has **Edit**, to correct your interest, and **Remove**, to take the property off your account.
